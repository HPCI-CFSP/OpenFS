# Worldwide Technology Survey

Use this Skill only for `MON-GLOBAL-TECH-001` discovery Work Items. Read the
pinned `config/global-technology-scope.json`, research baseline, Monitor, source
registry, acquisition policy, and assignment before searching.

## Coverage behavior

- Search the assigned technology question worldwide. Do not treat an English-language search or a known-vendor seed list as global coverage.
- Use primary records from the assigned region and source language where feasible. For a Japan-priority assignment, search Japanese and English and keep the same technical evaluation criteria used for international alternatives.
- Look for research, prototype, standardization, benchmark, deployment, procurement, production, maintenance, delay, discontinuation, and negative-result evidence. A product announcement alone does not establish maturity.
- Record region, organization type, technology category, maturity signal, language, source class, and positive or negative result in the capture metadata. Uncovered dimensions remain explicit Coverage gaps.
- Preserve original-publication lineage across translations, mirrors, press releases, and derivative articles. They are one Origin Group unless genuinely independent.

Follow the eligibility, bounded-passage, no-result, output, and stop rules in
`skills/source-discovery/SKILL.md`. Do not claim comprehensive Web coverage or
promote a newly observed technology directly; propose an additive Topic through
`OFS-004` after independent review.
