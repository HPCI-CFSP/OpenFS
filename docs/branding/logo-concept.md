# OpenFS Logo / ロゴコンセプト

Design: 125. Asset revision: 2026-08-30.

## 日本語

### 調べ、確かめ、未来を描き続ける。

OpenFSのロゴは、AIによる継続的な調査研究を、連なる二つの六角形のループで表現しています。一方は公開情報の「探索・収集」、もう一方は根拠に基づく「検証・統合」を象徴します。調査から得た知見が次の問いにつながり、知識と将来計画を更新し続ける循環を示しています。

完全には閉じていないループは、新しい情報や異なる視点を受け入れる、開かれた研究基盤を表しています。緑は新たな知見の発見、青は分析と検証を象徴し、グラデーションは理解が深まっていく過程を表現しています。

変化する技術動向を捉え、根拠を確かめながら、ロードマップやシステム整備計画を描き直していく。その継続性と開放性を、このロゴに込めています。

### 短い紹介文

連なる二つの開いた六角形は、AIによる「探索・収集」と「検証・統合」の循環を表しています。新しい知見を取り込み、根拠に基づいて知識と将来計画を更新し続ける、OpenFSの継続性と開放性を象徴しています。

## English

### Keep exploring, verifying, and shaping the future.

The OpenFS logo represents continuous AI-assisted research through two linked
hexagonal loops. One symbolizes the discovery and collection of public
information; the other represents evidence-based verification and synthesis.
Insights from each investigation lead to new questions, forming a cycle that
continually updates both knowledge and future plans.

The open loops represent a research platform that welcomes new information and
different perspectives. Green symbolizes the discovery of new insights, while
blue represents analysis and verification. The gradients express the process of
deepening understanding.

OpenFS follows changing technology trends, checks the evidence, and revisits
roadmaps and system planning options. The logo embodies this continuity and
openness.

### Short Description

Two linked, open hexagons represent the cycles of AI-assisted discovery and
collection, and verification and synthesis. They symbolize OpenFS's continuity
and openness: incorporating new insights and continually updating knowledge and
future plans on the basis of evidence.

### Usage and Maintenance

- The SVGs below are the source assets. Both READMEs reference the standard
  version, and the Pages home header uses the compact version. This document is
  the single source for the concept text; it is not published on Pages.
- Concept 125's colors, gradients, symbol geometry, and nine equal-width corner
  cuts are preserved. Use the same files on white and black backgrounds without
  recoloring or changing their aspect ratios.
- The Avenir Next Medium wordmark is outlined, so rendering does not require an
  installed font. No font files are included.
- Recommended minimum display sizes are 240px wide for the standard version,
  48px high for the compact version, and 32px square for the symbol. At 16px, fine
  corner cuts are difficult to distinguish.
- Keep filenames stable and use Git for revision history. Changes to the design
  or its meaning must update both languages in this document and the display
  tests together. The logo does not indicate that research has passed the
  Consensus Gate or completed verification.
