# Roadmap Principles

- Optimize for the HPCI ecosystem, not one system or benchmark in isolation.
- Present multiple coherent scenarios, including balanced, cost-constrained, power-constrained, and technology-accelerated cases where relevant.
- Label observations, assumptions, forecasts, decisions, and recommendations explicitly.
- Include dependencies, decision windows, trigger indicators, lead time, and fallback options.
- Preserve uncertainty and dissent instead of collapsing them into a single score.
- Distinguish no-regret preparatory actions from irreversible procurement commitments.
- Re-evaluate a Roadmap Item when a supporting Claim changes, an assumption expires, or a trigger threshold is crossed.
- Keep public-source and NDA-derived scenario work in their respective information planes.
- Evaluate benchmark importance with separate evidence for public implementation,
  published results, independent use, maintenance, workload relevance, and
  decision impact. Do not use media visibility or a paper citation alone as the
  priority score.

An accepted Roadmap Item identifies the responsible decision body and its supporting Decision IDs. Agent consensus may qualify an item for consideration but does not replace governance accountability.
