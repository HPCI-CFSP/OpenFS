# Report Guidelines

Reports are generated views, not the primary research record.

- Every material factual sentence links to accepted Claim IDs.
- Forecasts identify source, horizon, assumptions, and uncertainty.
- Recommendations identify scenario, evaluation criteria, Decision IDs, dissent, and accountable owner.
- Do not hide provisional or contested status through polished prose.
- Preserve measurement units, comparison baselines, and relevant dates.
- State search scope and known coverage gaps.
- Do not expose private identifiers or imply that public findings incorporate NDA material.
- A correction supersedes the prior export and records affected Claim and Decision IDs.

Draft generation can be automated. Public release, high-impact recommendations, and NDA-derived exports remain Level C actions.
