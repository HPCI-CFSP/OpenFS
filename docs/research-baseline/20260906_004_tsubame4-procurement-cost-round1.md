# TSUBAME4.0 procurement and five-year cost boundary

## Result

This provisional, single-model pass adds TSUBAME4.0 as the sixteenth case in
the public procurement register. The official award notice reports JPY
84,064,519 per month, and the corresponding tender notice gives a lease period
from April 1, 2024 through March 31, 2030. OpenFS therefore reproduces a
contractual known-cost floor of JPY 5,043,871,140 for the first 60 months.

The calculation is:

`84,064,519 JPY/month x 60 months = 5,043,871,140 JPY`

This is not complete five-year TCO. Tax treatment and inclusion of maintenance,
installation, software, electricity, cooling, shared facilities, staffing,
amendments, expansion, decommissioning, and migration remain unverified. The
public operating configuration is linked for context, but no publicly usable
final specification has been obtained to prove that every deployed component
is included in the monthly amount. Component prices are not inferred.

Primary sources:

- JETRO Government Procurement Database, [tender notice](https://www.jetro.go.jp/gov_procurement/national/articles/256628/2022121400400001.html)
- JETRO Government Procurement Database, [award notice](https://www.jetro.go.jp/gov_procurement/national/articles/272435/2023050100870131.html?media=pc)
- Institute of Science Tokyo, [FY2025 TSUBAME shared-use briefing](https://www.gsic.titech.ac.jp/sites/default/files/R7kobo3.pdf)

## 日本語要約

TSUBAME4.0を公共調達台帳の16件目として追加しました。公式の落札公示は月額
8,406万4,519円、対応する入札公告は2024年4月1日から2030年3月31日までの借入期間を
示します。このため、最初の60か月の契約上の既知費用下限を50億4,387万1,140円と
再計算できます。ただし税区分、保守、設置、ソフトウェア、電力、冷却、共用施設、
人件費、契約変更、増設、撤去、移行の包含は未確認であり、完全な5年間TCOではありません。
公開可能な最終仕様書も未取得のため、月額から部品単価を推定していません。単一モデルに
よる調査であり、Consensusは未完了です。
